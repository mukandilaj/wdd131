Kinshasa, Democratic Republic Of Congo

The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) is a vast Central African nation—Africa's second largest by area—with its capital in Kinshasa, over 100 million inhabitants, French as its official language, and immense mineral wealth including major cobalt, copper, and coltan reserves. The DRC spans diverse landscapes: dense Congo Basin rainforest, the world's second largest tropical rainforest; the Congo River, one of the world's deepest and most voluminous rivers; eastern highlands including the Great Rift Valley, volcanoes, and major lakes such as Lake Tanganyika and Lake Kivu.
